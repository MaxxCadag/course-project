Indie is short for Indepdent, and its a term used to describe how people fund their projects that are not backed by major corporate companies. Instead they are self-funded or crowdfunded projects with the sole purpose to produce a piece of media or product. Indie Catelog caters to the projects who are telling stories through animation, live action, comics, and films that the are made and funded by the creator themselves.
