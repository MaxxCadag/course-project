Our Mission

The catelog serves as a hub to share indie stories for those looking for something new instead of waiting for corporate studios to make something newer then their prequel and sequel movies of the same franchise. The Indie Catelog shares new indie projects of stories through different medias and we hope that this mission will keep indie sphere alive and inspire more creators to tell their stories.
